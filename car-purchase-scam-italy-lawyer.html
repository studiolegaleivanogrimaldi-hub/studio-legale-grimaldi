Car purchase scam in Italy: legal help for foreign buyers

Paid for a vehicle advertised by an Italian seller but never received it? An Italian lawyer can assess the documents, the payment trail and possible legal remedies.

Request a legal consultation

What to do immediately

Contact your sending bank without delay. Report suspected fraud and request a bank transfer recall. Preserve transaction references, beneficiary details and any written response. A recall does not guarantee that funds will be returned.

Check the seller and the payment beneficiary

A company registration or pro forma invoice does not prove that the seller possessed the car or authorized a private individual to collect payment. Check the registered company details, correspondence, invoice, beneficiary names, IBANs and any changes to payment instructions.

Can you file a criminal complaint from abroad?

Depending on the circumstances, a complaint concerning conduct in Italy may be filed through the competent Italian authorities. Italian criminal procedure also provides for presentation of a querela to an Italian consular officer abroad. The correct form, deadlines and jurisdiction require case-specific review.

Can money sent by bank transfer be recovered?

Possible steps include a bank recall, examination of applicable payment-service duties, criminal investigation and civil recovery proceedings. An authorized transfer induced by deception is not automatically reimbursed by the bank. Recovery depends on evidence, the recipient and the availability of funds.

Documents to prepare

Provide the pro forma invoice, purchase agreement, advertisement URL, vehicle identification number if available, bank transfer confirmations, beneficiary details and communications. Do not send passwords, one-time codes or unnecessary sensitive information.

Frequently asked questions

Is a pro forma invoice proof that the car was sold by the company?

Not by itself. Its origin, authenticity and connection to the payment instructions should be checked.

Do I have to travel to Italy to report the fraud?

Not necessarily. Available procedures depend on the facts and the form of complaint.

Will an Italian lawyer guarantee recovery?

No. The likelihood of recovery can only be assessed after reviewing the evidence and the payment trail.

A bank transfer recall or legal action does not guarantee recovery. The assessment depends on the payment records, applicable law and available evidence.
