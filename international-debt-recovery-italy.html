Recovering unpaid commercial invoices from Italian companies

For cross-border business claims, the initial assessment considers the contract, agreed jurisdiction, payment terms, evidence of delivery or performance, debtor identity and the prospects of enforcement in Italy.

Which procedure may be available?

Depending on the circumstances, options may include an Italian payment order (decreto ingiuntivo), a European Order for Payment for qualifying cross-border uncontested pecuniary claims, ordinary litigation or enforcement of an existing enforceable title. The European procedure is governed by Regulation (EC) No 1896/2006; it is not suitable for every dispute.

Information for an initial confidential enquiry

Provide the countries of the parties, approximate amount, nature of the commercial relationship and relevant deadlines. Please do not send confidential documents in the first message. Fees and the scope of work are agreed before any engagement.
