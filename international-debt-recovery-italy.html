Debt Recovery in Italy for Foreign Companies and Individuals

Italian legal assistance for foreign businesses and private creditors seeking payment from debtors in Italy.

Unpaid invoices and commercial debts

Assessment of supply agreements, invoices, payment demands and legal action against Italian companies or professionals.

Personal loans and private debts

Assistance with documented loans, unpaid contractual obligations and repayment claims against individuals in Italy.

Italian and European payment orders

Assessment of Italian payment order proceedings and the European Order for Payment under Regulation (EC) No 1896/2006, where applicable.

Enforcement and insolvency

Assistance with enforcing judgments and payment orders against assets in Italy and asserting creditor claims in insolvency proceedings.

How to request an assessment

Send a brief description, the creditor’s and debtor’s countries, the amount claimed and the available documents. We will assess jurisdiction, the appropriate procedure and engagement terms before undertaking work.

The availability of each procedure depends on jurisdiction, the applicable law and the evidence. Results cannot be guaranteed.
