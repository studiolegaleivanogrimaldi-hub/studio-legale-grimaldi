European order for payment in Italy

A cross-border procedure for uncontested monetary claims under Regulation (EC) No 1896/2006.

What we assess

We assess whether the claim is a due and quantified monetary claim in a cross-border civil or commercial matter, the competent court and the supporting documents.

Eligibility requires a qualifying cross-border civil or commercial monetary claim; Denmark does not participate. The defendant may lodge a statement of opposition within the prescribed period.

Cross-border considerations

Jurisdiction, applicable law, service abroad, limitation periods and enforcement must be assessed case by case.
