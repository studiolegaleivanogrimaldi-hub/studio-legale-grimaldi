A connection with Italy does not automatically establish jurisdiction of an Italian court or entitlement to a residence permit. The competent authority and applicable law depend on the individual facts.

Residence permits and administrative refusals

Eligibility depends on the purpose of entry or stay and the applicant’s individual circumstances.

A refusal must be reviewed for the competent authority, notification date, appeal deadline and available procedure; there is no single remedy for every immigration decision.

The firm can assess Italian-law requirements and represent clients before competent Italian authorities where appropriate.

Single work permit: 2026 update

Legislative Decree No. 83 of 16 April 2026, effective from 4 June 2026, implements Directive (EU) 2024/1233 on the single application procedure for residence and work. Applicable deadlines and conditions depend on the permit category; the reform does not apply uniformly to every residence permit.
