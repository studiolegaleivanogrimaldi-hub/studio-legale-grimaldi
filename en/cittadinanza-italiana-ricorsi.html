Citizenship applications and challenges

Citizenship by marriage, residence and descent are separate legal routes with different requirements.

Claims based on Italian descent must be assessed under the legislative changes introduced in 2025, including any applicable transitional provisions.

Delays and refusals may call for different administrative or judicial remedies depending on the circumstances.

Italian citizenship: legislative update

Law No. 91 of 5 February 1992, as amended by Decree-Law No. 36 of 28 March 2025, converted into Law No. 74 of 23 May 2025, includes Article 3-bis, which limits citizenship by descent for certain persons born abroad who hold another nationality, subject to statutory exceptions. Application dates, pending court proceedings, ancestors’ citizenship and a parent’s residence in Italy must be assessed individually.
