A connection with Italy does not automatically establish jurisdiction of an Italian court or entitlement to a residence permit. The competent authority and applicable law depend on the individual facts.

Investor and business immigration routes

Investor Visa for Italy is a specific programme subject to statutory investment categories and thresholds; purchasing property alone does not automatically qualify.

Self-employment, innovative start-up and investor routes have distinct eligibility criteria and procedures.

Incorporating an Italian company does not automatically confer immigration status; tax and corporate implications may also require specialist advice.

Investor visa requirements

Article 26-bis of Legislative Decree No. 286/1998 governs Italy’s investor visa. The investment thresholds vary by route: EUR 250,000 for innovative startups, EUR 500,000 for Italian companies, EUR 2 million for government bonds, or EUR 1 million for qualifying philanthropic donations. Eligibility, source of funds, nationality-related restrictions and current administrative guidance must be checked before applying.
