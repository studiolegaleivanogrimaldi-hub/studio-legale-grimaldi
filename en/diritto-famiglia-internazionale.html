International family law in Italy

Legal assistance in Italy for families living abroad.

Issues to assess

  • Jurisdiction and applicable law in cross-border family disputes
  • Recognition of foreign family judgments in Italy
  • Family assets and parental responsibilities connected with Italy
A connection with Italy does not automatically establish jurisdiction. The competent authority and applicable law depend on the facts.

Cross-border issues to assess

Habitual residence, nationality and the location of assets may affect jurisdiction, but no single connecting factor determines every issue.

Within its scope, Regulation (EU) 2019/1111 addresses jurisdiction, recognition and enforcement in matrimonial and parental responsibility matters.

The law governing divorce, maintenance and matrimonial property may differ; each issue requires a separate assessment.

EU rules and scope

Regulation (EU) 2019/1111 governs jurisdiction and cross-border recognition in matrimonial and parental responsibility matters within its scope, generally for proceedings instituted from 1 August 2022; it does not govern maintenance obligations. Regulation (EC) No. 4/2009 addresses maintenance in relevant EU cases. Matrimonial property requires separate consideration under Regulation (EU) 2016/1103, applicable in participating Member States from 29 January 2019, with transitional rules, particularly concerning applicable law.
