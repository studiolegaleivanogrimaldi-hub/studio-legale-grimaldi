Buying Property in Italy: Italian Real Estate Lawyer for Foreign Buyers

Planning to purchase a house, apartment, holiday home or investment property in Italy? Avv. Ivano Grimaldi, an Italian lawyer based in Catania, provides legal assistance to buyers living abroad, including clients in the United States, United Kingdom and Canada.

Legal advice can help you understand contractual commitments and potential risks before you sign a purchase offer or preliminary agreement. Initial consultations can be arranged remotely.

Legal assistance throughout the purchase

  • Purchase offers: review of proposed terms, deposits, deadlines and binding commitments.
  • Preliminary contracts (compromesso): examination of clauses, conditions, remedies and buyer protections.
  • Property title and registered encumbrances: legal assessment of ownership records, mortgages, seizures and other relevant restrictions, based on available documentation and searches.
  • Planning and cadastral matters: coordination with qualified surveyors and technical professionals for necessary checks.
  • Completion before a notary: contractual assistance and coordination with the Italian notary responsible for the deed.
  • Disputes: legal assistance if contractual problems arise before or after completion.

Buying Italian property from the United States or abroad

Foreign buyers may need to consider identification and tax-code requirements, the applicable rules on purchasing Italian real estate, financing, documents signed abroad and the use of powers of attorney. Eligibility and formalities depend on nationality, residency and the circumstances of the transaction.

Our firm can review the legal aspects of a proposed purchase and coordinate, where necessary, with notaries, tax advisers and technical professionals. Notarial certification, tax advice and technical surveys remain within the responsibilities of the respective qualified professionals.

Why seek legal advice before signing?

A property purchase offer may create binding obligations even before the final notarial deed. Reviewing the documentation early may help identify legal risks, clarify conditions and avoid misunderstandings over deposits, completion dates and the seller's obligations.

Frequently asked questions

Can a US citizen buy property in Italy?

Possibly, but the legal requirements must be checked in the specific case, including any applicable reciprocity rules, residence status and exceptions. A legal assessment should be completed before committing to a purchase.

Do I need to travel to Italy to buy a property?

Some steps may be managed remotely. In appropriate cases, a valid power of attorney can allow representation for specific formalities, subject to the notary's requirements.

Is an Italian notary enough, or should I consult a lawyer?

The notary performs an independent public function in the conveyancing process. A lawyer can separately advise you on your interests, contractual negotiations and legal risks.

Can the firm assist with property purchases outside Sicily?

Initial legal advice can be provided remotely for proposed purchases elsewhere in Italy. The scope of further assistance and any necessary local coordination will be assessed case by case.
