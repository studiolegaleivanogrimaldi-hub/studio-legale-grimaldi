Legal assistance for inherited property and inheritance rights in Italy

Inheritance in Italy for heirs living abroad

Avv. Ivano Grimaldi is an Italian lawyer based in Catania. The firm assists people living outside Italy with legal issues concerning inheritances, inherited property and disputes between heirs in Italy. Initial consultations may be arranged remotely.

How we can help

  • Determining inheritance rights and the shares due to heirs.
  • Inherited houses, land and other assets located in Italy.
  • Disputes and division of estates involving heirs in different countries.
  • Protection of reserved inheritance shares and challenges to wills where legally justified.
  • Acceptance or renunciation of inheritance and assessment of estate debts.
  • Legal issues that must be resolved before inherited Italian property can be sold.

Important information

Cross-border inheritance matters require an assessment of applicable law, jurisdiction and relevant procedures. Notarial, tax and foreign-law issues may require appropriately qualified local professionals.
